Loom

Two introductions to the same tool. Same engine, same controls — they differ only in how you are let in.

Draft 1 The cover

The name sits over the field, which shuffles through all five views on its own — the sphere turning, the strip running. The nine colours underneath are the legend and the first control at once: touch one and every line carrying that meaning comes forward. Three lines say what you can do; Explore slides the panel in from the left.

 Draft 2 The snap

A card in the corner that already works: push a row to change the view or the order, watch the field answer. Hovering Explore shows where the card is going; pressing it walks the same card to the left edge, unfolds it into the full panel, and slides the field across into what is left.

Five views — Nodes, Timeline, Clusters, Grid, Sphere — each sorted by Colour, Meaning or Frequency. The sort is also what the grouped views group and name their columns by, so the names change with it. The lines are the meanings of the grids: a line's colour says what two pictures share, and picking one meaning leaves just those pictures and the ties between them.
