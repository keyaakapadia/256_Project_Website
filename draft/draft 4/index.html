THE LATTICE — THREE RELATIONSHIPS, AT THE COST OF SEEING ANY OF THEM
A flat grid holds 256 items in full view but can only carry two relationships. A lattice carries three — and immediately hides most of itself behind itself. Slicing is how you pay for the third axis.
There is exactly one slot per address, so items that want the same cell cannot all have it. They are pushed to the nearest free slot and the displacement is drawn. The grid does not describe the collection; it deforms it to fit.
